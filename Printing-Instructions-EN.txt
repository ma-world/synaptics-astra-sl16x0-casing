Astra Machina SL1680 | Enclosure for the complete development kit

The assembled enclosure measures 119.2 × 88 × 41.4 mm. It fits the
100 × 79 mm SL16X0 I/O board and SL1680 core module. The printed base
replaces the stock carrier frame if one is fitted. There is 27.8 mm of
clearance from the top of the I/O board to the underside of the lid.

1. Print Lochschablone.stl first and check its four standoffs against the
   mounting holes in the I/O board. The hole spacing is 92 × 58 mm.
2. Print a base and one lid in the supplied orientation. For Boden-WLAN.stl,
   print WLAN-Klappe.stl as well. Suggested starting settings are PETG,
   0.2 mm layers, and 3 walls. For the Wi-Fi access base, check the short
   bridges at the two hatch screw mounts in your slicer.
3. Secure the I/O board with four M2.5 × 6 mm screws. Fit the lid with
   four M3 × 8 mm screws. Both sets of screws thread into printed pilot holes.

The side walls are 2 mm thick and the lid top is 2.4 mm thick. The base
floor is 2.6 mm thick and 2 mm thick under the antenna pockets. The
screw posts sit 0.5 mm from the adjacent lid wall. An already printed
119.2 mm wide base can be reused with any lid variant. The existing
mounting template still fits.

Boden.stl is the standard base. Boden-Antennen-Innen.stl has two rectangular
antenna pockets alongside the floor vents. Each pocket is 52 × 12 mm and
3.5 mm deep. Hold the antennas in place with thin double-sided tape and
route the coax cables through the open slots toward the centre. Connect
them to the Wi-Fi card before fastening the I/O board. All lid variants fit
this base. Wi-Fi reception in this antenna position has yet to be checked.

Boden-WLAN.stl retains the antenna pockets and adds an access opening of
roughly 53 × 34 mm. Fit WLAN-Klappe.stl from below with its lift notch
toward the main port row. Fasten it with two M3 × 6 mm countersunk screws.
For service, disconnect power, remove the two screws, and lift the hatch
at the notch. The opening position is based on the Synaptics drawing.

Deckel.stl is the standard lid. Deckel-GPIO-12mm.stl has a 63.8 × 12 mm opening
above the 40-pin GPIO header (J32). The pins sit below the lid surface. If
needed, feed the cable through the opening and plug it in before fastening
the lid. Print the lid with its outer face on the build plate. When fitting
it, align the wide front opening with the carrier's main port row. The
opening is lower over USB-C and HDMI than over Ethernet and the stacked
USB-A ports. The rear microSD opening is 16.8 mm high.

Deckel-Kameraseite-Zu.stl and Deckel-GPIO-12mm-Kameraseite-Zu.stl close
the camera-side wall of the lid. Route the ribbon cable through the
5.8 mm high opening in the base before fastening the lid.

The mounting hole spacing has been checked on an SL1680 kit using the
template. Check access for your plugs and clearance above your heatsink.
You can adjust the dimensions, pilot holes, GPIO opening, and antenna
pockets in the OpenSCAD source.
